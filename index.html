Leela

Attention. Silence. Community. Play. Wildness. Ritual. Wonder. Time outside. Time together. Working with our hands. Learning from elders. Sitting around fires. Looking at stars. Being unreachable for a while.

Leela is a place in the foothills outside Lyons, Colorado, set aside for these things. Come to practice, to gather, to make, to rest, or simply to spend unhurried time on the land.

The idea

Modern life has given us extraordinary things. Along the way, we have begun to lose others.

Leela begins with a simple question: what does the world need more of? The answers are rarely new. Silence. Shared meals. Time outdoors. Craft. Conversation across generations. The experience of being fully present somewhere.

Leela exists to make physical space for them. It is a retreat center, a contemplative sanctuary, a wild place, a creative community, and a place to gather. It is a place for remembering what it means to be human.

The land

Simple. Beautiful. Enough.

Leela begins modestly, with a house, a few yurts, and a camp. It is rustic by intention. Care is given where it matters: good bedding, warm light, clean spaces, good food, and things made by hand.

The House

The communal heart of Leela. Kitchen, dining table, library, practice room, and fireplace.

The Yurts

Simple, beautiful accommodations set within the landscape.

The Camp

Thoughtfully placed campsites for those who prefer to sleep closer to the ground.

Access

Deeper experiences sustain simpler ones.

Retreats, accommodations, and workshops sustain the land. They also make it possible to offer free morning meditation, community dinners, scholarships, residencies, and work-trade.

Those who are able to take part in deeper paid experiences help make simpler community experiences available to everyone.
