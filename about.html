The idea

What does the world need more of?

This is the question behind Leela. Leela is an attempt to give the answers a physical place.

What is being lost

Modern life has given us extraordinary things. Somewhere along the way, we have begun losing others, mostly without noticing and rarely by choice.

Attention. Play. Community. Silence. Wildness. Creativity. Ritual. Movement. Wonder. Boredom. Time outside. Time together. Working with our hands. Learning from elders. Sitting around fires. Looking at stars. Doing things we are not good at. Knowing our neighbors. Being unreachable for a while.

Leela exists to create a place where these can flourish again. It is a place for remembering some of what it means to be human.

What Leela is

Not one thing, but something of several.

Leela is not a yoga retreat, a spa, a monastery, a summer camp, an artist colony, or a wilderness school. It holds something of each.

It is a nature-based retreat center, a contemplative sanctuary, a wild place, a creative community, and a gathering place. One might picture a monastery, a wilderness school, an artist colony, and an old mountain lodge sharing the same land.

Three movements

To yourself, to each other, to the world.

Beneath all of Leela's programs are three movements. Leela exists where they meet.

Come back to yourself

The morning sit, the unscheduled afternoon, the bench at the edge of the meadow. Time to notice what one notices.

Attention · Silence · Rest · Meditation · Practice · Embodiment · Reflection · Boredom · Presence

Come back to each other

The shared table, the fire circle, the work of cleaning up after a meal. The places where strangers become neighbors.

Community · Play · Meals · Storytelling · Celebration · Service · Elders · Conversation · Music · Laughter

Come back to the world

The ridge, the garden, the woodpile, the first snow, and a night sky that is still fully visible here.

Nature · Wildness · Making · Stewardship · Seasons · Animals · Weather · Wonder · Stars

Character

Held in balance.

Leela aims to be warm and quiet, serious without being solemn. Above all, it should feel human.

Spiritual life

Open, curious, and respectful.

Leela is spiritually open and non-denominational. Buddhist practice and the dharma are meaningful influences. Buddhist imagery is present on the land, and Buddhist teachers lead many of our meditation retreats.

The land also holds sacred art from other traditions, secular and folk art, sculpture, natural objects, and work made by guests and resident artists. Each tradition is honored on its own terms rather than blended into the others.

No belief is required. A person may spend three days at Leela without subscribing to any particular spiritual view.

Technology and attention

A place where nothing is competing for your attention.

Leela is not opposed to technology. Technology is useful. But human attention deserves places where nothing is trying to capture it.

Many gatherings are held without phones. The practice room, library, and dining table are screen-free, and some retreats include a full digital sabbath. Cell coverage on the land is limited, and we consider that part of the place.

The physical place

Simple. Beautiful. Enough.

Leela begins with modest means, and parts of it are rustic. This is not something to conceal. It is part of the character of the place.

Quality is given where it counts: good bedding, warm lighting, thoughtful architecture, beautiful objects, clean spaces, good food, and things made by hand. Comfort, without unnecessary luxury. Leela should feel intentional rather than expensive.

A clear mind

A substance-free environment.

Leela is a clear-minded, substance-free property. We ask that guests and visitors leave alcohol and recreational drugs at home.
