Calendar

Autumn and early winter.

Some offerings recur each week. Others arrive once a year with the turning of the season. All are open to newcomers.

Each week

The weekly rhythm.

Wed–Sun · 7:00 am

Morning practice

Meditation, gentle movement, and tea. Offered freely.

Wed–Sun · 10 am–4 pm

Land hours

The trails, library, and porch are open to visitors.

Thursdays · 6:30 pm

Community dinner

A shared meal at one table. By donation.

Saturdays · 9 am–12 pm

Land day

Garden, trail, and woodpile work, followed by lunch.

Sundays · 4 pm

Open studio

Materials and tables for drawing, clay, and handwork.

Mondays and Tuesdays

Rest days

No programs are held. The land rests.

Upcoming

Programs and gatherings.

September

  1. 26Sat · 6 pm

    Gathering

    Autumn Equinox Fire

    A fire and shared meal to mark the balance of light and dark. Families welcome.

    Free · please bring a dish

October

  1. 1Thu · 6:30 pm

    Gathering

    Community Dinner

    The first autumn soup, bread from the oven, and one long table.

    By donation

  2. 4Sun · 10 am

    Practice

    Dharma Talk: The Practice of Attention

    A talk and discussion with a visiting teacher, followed by lunch. Open to newcomers and experienced practitioners.

    By donation

  3. 9–11Fri–Sun

    Retreat

    Three Days of Silence

    A silent meditation retreat of sitting and walking practice, simple meals, and time on the land. Led by a visiting dharma teacher.

    From $385 with lodging · scholarships available

  4. 14Wed · 7 pm

    Making

    Drawing for Non-Artists

    An evening of drawing for those who do not draw. No experience required, and no skill expected.

    $10 suggested · materials provided

  5. 17Sat · 9 am

    Wilderness

    Plants of the Foothills

    A slow foraging walk with a longtime resident of the Lyons area: what is edible, what is medicinal, and what to leave alone.

    Sliding scale $15–40

  6. 22Thu · 7:30 pm

    Gathering

    Storytelling by the Fire

    Short, true, personal stories told aloud. Come to tell one, or to listen.

    Free

  7. 25Sun · 2 pm

    Gathering

    Death Café

    An unhurried conversation about mortality, over tea. Gentle, open, and without agenda.

    Free

  8. 31Sat · 5 pm

    Gathering

    Remembrance Fire

    An evening to remember those we have lost. Bring a name, a photograph, or simply yourself.

    By donation

November

  1. 6–8Fri–Sun

    Wilderness

    Fire, Water, Shelter

    A weekend course in foundational wilderness skills: friction fire, finding and treating water, and building shelter. Camping included.

    $295 · camping included

  2. 12Thu · 6 pm

    Making

    Spoon Carving with an Elder

    A retired carpenter teaches the carving of a simple wooden spoon. Tools and wood provided.

    Sliding scale $20–50

  3. 15Sun · 10 am

    Family

    Family Day on the Land

    Tracking, exploration, and a fire, for children and the adults who accompany them.

    Free · registration requested

  4. 21Sat · 7 pm

    Gathering

    House Concert

    An acoustic evening of folk song beside the fireplace.

    $15–25

  5. 26Thu · 2 pm

    Gathering

    Thanksgiving at the Long Table

    A shared supper for anyone who would like a place to be. Help with the cooking is welcome.

    By donation

December

  1. 4–6Fri–Sun

    Retreat

    Digital Sabbath Retreat

    Two days without devices. Rest, walking, shared meals, reading, and practice.

    From $345 with lodging

  2. 10Thu · 6 pm

    Making

    An Introduction to Clay

    Hand-building in clay for beginners. The aim is the making, not the result.

    $35 · materials included

  3. 21Mon · 4 pm

    Gathering

    Winter Solstice

    A silent sit at sunset, followed by lanterns, a fire, and a shared meal on the longest night of the year. Held on a rest day by exception.

    Free · all welcome

Nothing of this kind is scheduled at present. Morning practice is held Wednesday through Sunday.

Hosting

Offering a program at Leela.

Teachers, artists, and small groups occasionally host retreats and workshops at Leela. If your work is aligned with the spirit of the place, we would be glad to hear from you.

Please describe what you have in mind, who it is for, and the season you are considering. hosting@leela.place

Leela

A place to be human.

A retreat center and community sanctuary in the foothills outside Lyons, Colorado. A clear-minded, substance-free place.
