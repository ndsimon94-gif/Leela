Staying at Leela

Getting here

In the foothills above Lyons.

Leela lies just outside Lyons, Colorado, where the plains meet the mountains. It is about 25 minutes from Boulder, an hour from Denver, and roughly 75 minutes from Denver International Airport.

Navigation apps can be unreliable for the final mile. Please follow the signs from the county road.

The road

The final stretch is gravel and suitable for most vehicles. In winter, all-wheel drive or snow tires are recommended.

Shared rides

For retreats and programs, we help guests arrange shared rides from Boulder and Denver.

What to bring

Prepared for changing weather.

Conditions in the foothills change quickly. A warm afternoon can become a cold evening within the hour, and summer storms are common in the afternoon.

  • A warm layer and a rain shell, in every season
  • Footwear suited to uneven ground
  • A water bottle; the air is dry at this elevation
  • A headlamp, if staying after dark
  • A book, a notebook, or nothing at all

Guidelines

How we share the land.

Attention

The practice room, library, and dining table are screen-free, and many gatherings are held without phones. A place to leave devices is provided at the House.

Clarity

Leela is a substance-free property. Please leave alcohol and recreational drugs at home.

Quiet

Quiet hours are observed from 9:30 pm to 7:00 am. Some retreats keep silence throughout the day, and this is indicated where it applies.

Care

Each person helps care for the place: washing a cup, tending a shared space, carrying wood when the pile is low. Leave Leela a little better than you found it.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Do I need to be Buddhist, or religious at all?

No. Buddhist practice is a meaningful influence at Leela, and many of our meditation teachers come from the dharma. Leela is non-denominational, however, and no belief is required. Many people come for the quiet, the land, and the company.

I have never meditated. May I attend morning practice?

Yes. Please arrive a few minutes early and someone will offer brief instruction. Chairs are always available.

May I bring my dog?

Dogs are welcome in designated areas and at certain events, which are noted on the calendar. Please keep dogs leashed and outside the House, the practice room, and the gardens.

Is Leela suitable for children and families?

Leela is primarily oriented toward adults. Family days and family programs are offered throughout the year and are marked on the calendar. Children are also welcome at seasonal gatherings such as the equinox and solstice.

Is alcohol permitted?

Leela is a clear-minded, substance-free environment. We ask that no alcohol or recreational drugs be brought onto the property.

Is there cell service?

Coverage on the land is limited. Staff can always be reached, and a phone is available at the House in case of emergency.

What if I cannot afford a retreat?

Morning practice and many gatherings are free. Most workshops are offered on a sliding scale. Scholarships are available for every retreat, and work-trade offers another way to stay.

About access
How rustic is Leela?

Leela is simple, and cared for. Beds are comfortable, lighting is warm, and food is prepared with care. Paths are gravel, washrooms are a short walk from the yurts, and the weather is part of daily life.

Is the land accessible?

The House can be reached from the parking area on a firm, level path. Much of the land is uneven and hilly. Please tell us about your needs in advance, and we will do our best to accommodate them.

May I come simply to do nothing?

Yes. That is one of the things Leela is for.
