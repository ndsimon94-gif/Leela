On pricing

Pay what fits.

Prices are set so that Leela can endure. We also recognize that those who most need a place like this are often least able to pay for it.

For that reason, most programs are offered on a sliding scale. Paying at the higher end helps someone else attend. The lower end exists for those who need it, and no explanation is asked.

Ways to take part

Everyone contributes something.

Scholarships

Partial and full scholarships are available for every retreat. We ask only for a short note about why you would like to attend.

Work-trade

Offer time in the kitchen, garden, or on the trails in exchange for lodging, meals, or retreat tuition. Stays range from a weekend to a full season.

Volunteering

Join a Saturday land day, help prepare a community dinner, or offer a skill. Carpenters and gardeners are especially welcome.

Teaching

If you know a craft, carry stories, or remember how things were once done, we would be glad for you to share it. Elders are especially welcome.

Giving

Gifts of any size support community access directly: free programs, scholarships, and residencies.
